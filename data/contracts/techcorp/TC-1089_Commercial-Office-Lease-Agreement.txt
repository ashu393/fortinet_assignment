                              RECITALS

WHEREAS, Party B owns certain commercial real estate and Party A desires to lease office space for its business operations

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein
contained, the parties agree as follows:

================================================================================
                         TERMS AND CONDITIONS
================================================================================

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES/PRODUCTS

Party B leases to Party A approximately 10,000 square feet of office space located at:
Floor 12, Building A
123 Tech Plaza
San Francisco, CA 94102

Premises include:
- Open office area and private offices
- Conference rooms and collaborative spaces
- Kitchen and break room facilities
- 20 dedicated parking spaces in building garage
- Access to building amenities (gym, cafeteria)
- 24/7 building access for employees

2. TERM AND DURATION

2.1 Effective Date: This Agreement shall commence on January 1, 2024
    and shall continue until December 31, 2028 ("Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal: Party A has the option to renew for one (1) additional five-year term by providing written notice at least one hundred eighty (180) days prior to expiration. Renewal rent shall be at fair market value, not to exceed 110% of the then-current rent.

3. PAYMENT TERMS

Party A shall pay base rent of $20,000 per month plus operating expenses estimated at $5,000 per month.

3.1 Total Contract Value: $1,200,000 USD (5-year term)

3.2 Payment Schedule: Monthly rent due on the first day of each month. Operating expenses reconciled annually. First and last month's rent plus security deposit of $50,000 due upon lease execution.

3.3 Late Payment: Any payment not received within thirty (30) days of the due
    date shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Definition: "Confidential Information" means all non-public information
    disclosed by either party, including but not limited to business plans,
    technical data, customer lists, and financial information.

4.2 Obligations: Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other
    party's Confidential Information and not disclose it to third parties without
    prior written consent.

4.3 Duration: Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this
    Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Not applicable to real estate lease.

6. WARRANTIES AND REPRESENTATIONS

6.1 Party A Warranties: Party A warrants that it has the full right and authority
    to enter into this Agreement and perform its obligations hereunder.

6.2 Party B Warranties: Party B warrants that the premises comply with all applicable building codes, zoning laws, and ADA requirements, and are suitable for general office use.

7. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

Party A shall indemnify Party B from claims arising from Party A's use of the premises. Party B shall indemnify Party A from claims arising from Party B's negligence in maintaining the building common areas.

7.1 Limitation of Liability: Each party's liability for property damage shall be limited to the amount recoverable under its insurance policies, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

7.2 Indemnification: Each party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other
    party from any claims, damages, or expenses arising from its breach of this
    Agreement or negligent acts.

8. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement:
a) For convenience by providing sixty (60) days written notice after the third anniversary of the Effective Date
b) Immediately for material breach if not cured within thirty (30) days of notice
c) By Party A if the premises become unusable due to casualty or condemnation

Early termination by Party A without cause before the third anniversary requires payment of a termination fee equal to three (3) months' rent.

8.1 Termination Notice Period: 60 days

8.2 Effect of Termination: Upon termination, each party shall:
    a) Return or destroy all Confidential Information
    b) Pay all outstanding amounts due
    c) Cooperate in transitioning services (if applicable)

9. FORCE MAJEURE

If the premises are damaged by fire, flood, earthquake, or other casualty making them unsuitable for occupancy, Party B shall repair within ninety (90) days or either party may terminate the lease. Rent shall abate during the period the premises are unusable.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.

10.2 Dispute Resolution: Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall first be
     attempted to be resolved through good faith negotiations. If negotiations fail,
     disputes shall be resolved through mediation administered by the San Francisco Mediation Service, followed by litigation if mediation is unsuccessful.

10.3 Venue: Exclusive venue for any legal proceedings shall be in San Francisco County, California.

11. MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between
     the parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

11.2 Amendments: This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

11.3 Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written
     consent of the other party.

11.4 Notices: All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to
     the addresses set forth above.

11.5 Severability: If any provision is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions
     shall continue in full force and effect.
