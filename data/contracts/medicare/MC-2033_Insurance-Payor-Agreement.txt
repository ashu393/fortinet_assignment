                              RECITALS
================================================================================

WHEREAS, Party A provides healthcare services and Party B operates a health insurance plan, and the parties wish to establish terms for Party A to provide covered services to Party B's members

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein
contained, the parties agree as follows:

================================================================================
                         TERMS AND CONDITIONS
================================================================================

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES/PRODUCTS

Party A shall provide medically necessary healthcare services to Party B's insured members including:
- Primary care services
- Specialist consultations
- Diagnostic testing and imaging
- Outpatient procedures
- Emergency care
- Preventive care services

Party A agrees to be a participating provider in Party B's network.

2. TERM AND DURATION

2.1 Effective Date: This Agreement shall commence on January 1, 2024
    and shall continue until December 31, 2026 ("Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal: This Agreement automatically renews for successive one-year terms unless either party provides one hundred eighty (180) days written notice of non-renewal. Payment rates may be renegotiated annually with mutual consent.

3. PAYMENT TERMS

Party B shall reimburse Party A for covered services according to the fee schedule attached as Exhibit A. Payment rates are as follows:
- Primary care visits: 85% of Medicare rates
- Specialist care: 90% of Medicare rates
- Diagnostic procedures: 80% of Medicare rates
- Emergency services: 100% of Medicare rates

Party B shall also pay quality bonus incentives based on performance metrics.

3.1 Total Contract Value: Variable based on claims (estimated $2M annually)

3.2 Payment Schedule: Party B shall pay clean claims within thirty (30) days of receipt. Disputed claims shall be resolved within sixty (60) days.

3.3 Late Payment: Any payment not received within thirty (30) days of the due
    date shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Definition: "Confidential Information" means all non-public information
    disclosed by either party, including but not limited to business plans,
    technical data, customer lists, and financial information.

4.2 Obligations: Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other
    party's Confidential Information and not disclose it to third parties without
    prior written consent.

4.3 Duration: Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this
    Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Not applicable to payor agreement.

6. WARRANTIES AND REPRESENTATIONS

6.1 Party A Warranties: Party A warrants that it has the full right and authority
    to enter into this Agreement and perform its obligations hereunder.

6.2 Party B Warranties: Party A warrants that:
a) It maintains all required licenses and certifications
b) It maintains professional liability insurance of at least $3 million per occurrence
c) It complies with all applicable healthcare regulations
d) It shall not balance bill Party B's members for covered services

Party B warrants that it shall process and pay clean claims in accordance with state prompt payment laws.

7. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

Each party shall indemnify the other from claims arising from its own negligence or breach of this Agreement. Party A is solely responsible for medical malpractice claims. Party B is responsible for coverage determination errors.

7.1 Limitation of Liability: Neither party's liability is capped for claims related to patient care or coverage disputes.

7.2 Indemnification: Each party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other
    party from any claims, damages, or expenses arising from its breach of this
    Agreement or negligent acts.

8. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement:
a) For convenience by providing one hundred eighty (180) days written notice
b) For cause with sixty (60) days notice if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within thirty (30) days
c) Immediately if the other party loses required licenses or regulatory approval
d) Immediately if the other party commits fraud or files for bankruptcy

Upon termination, Party A shall continue to provide care for members currently under treatment for up to ninety (90) days (continuity of care period).

8.1 Termination Notice Period: 180 days

8.2 Effect of Termination: Upon termination, each party shall:
    a) Return or destroy all Confidential Information
    b) Pay all outstanding amounts due
    c) Cooperate in transitioning services (if applicable)

9. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party shall be liable for delays in performance due to force majeure, provided that Party A shall continue to provide emergency care regardless of circumstances. If force majeure continues for more than sixty (60) days, either party may terminate with thirty (30) days notice.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois and applicable federal healthcare regulations.

10.2 Dispute Resolution: Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall first be
     attempted to be resolved through good faith negotiations. If negotiations fail,
     disputes shall be resolved through good faith negotiations followed by mediation administered by the American Health Lawyers Association, and if unresolved, binding arbitration.

10.3 Venue: Exclusive venue for any legal proceedings shall be in Chicago, Illinois.

11. MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between
     the parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

11.2 Amendments: This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

11.3 Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written
     consent of the other party.

11.4 Notices: All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to
     the addresses set forth above.

11.5 Severability: If any provision is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions
     shall continue in full force and effect.
