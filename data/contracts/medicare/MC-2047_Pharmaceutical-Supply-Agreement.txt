WHEREAS, Party A operates medical facilities requiring pharmaceutical products and Party B distributes pharmaceutical products to healthcare providers

contained, the parties agree as follows:

================================================================================
                         TERMS AND CONDITIONS
================================================================================

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES/PRODUCTS

Party B shall supply Party A with pharmaceutical products including:
- Brand name and generic medications
- Controlled substances (with appropriate DEA documentation)
- Vaccines and biologics
- Emergency medications
- Compounded preparations (as needed)

All products shall be FDA-approved and supplied with appropriate documentation including lot numbers, expiration dates, and chain of custody records.

2. TERM AND DURATION

2.1 Effective Date: This Agreement shall commence on February 1, 2024
    and shall continue until May 15, 2025 ("Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal: This Agreement may be renewed by mutual written consent. Party A has the right of first refusal if Party B changes its terms or pricing structure.

3. PAYMENT TERMS

Party A shall pay Party B based on the pricing schedule in Exhibit A, representing competitive wholesale acquisition cost (WAC) pricing with volume discounts applied.

3.1 Total Contract Value: $450,000 USD (estimated annual volume)

3.2 Payment Schedule: Invoices issued twice monthly based on deliveries. Payment due within thirty (30) days of invoice date via wire transfer or ACH.

3.3 Late Payment: Any payment not received within thirty (30) days of the due
    date shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Definition: "Confidential Information" means all non-public information
    disclosed by either party, including but not limited to business plans,
    technical data, customer lists, and financial information.

4.2 Obligations: Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other
    party's Confidential Information and not disclose it to third parties without
    prior written consent.

4.3 Duration: Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this
    Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Party B retains no intellectual property rights in pharmaceutical products, which are manufactured by third parties. All trademarks belong to their respective manufacturers.

6. WARRANTIES AND REPRESENTATIONS

6.1 Party A Warranties: Party A warrants that it has the full right and authority
    to enter into this Agreement and perform its obligations hereunder.

6.2 Party B Warranties: Party B warrants that:
a) All products are genuine, FDA-approved, and obtained through legitimate supply chains
b) Products are stored and transported under appropriate conditions (temperature, humidity, etc.)
c) Products shall have at least six (6) months shelf life remaining at time of delivery
d) Party B maintains all required DEA licenses for controlled substance distribution
e) Products are free from adulteration, contamination, and defects

7. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

Party B shall indemnify Party A from claims arising from defective or counterfeit products supplied by Party B. Party B shall maintain product liability insurance of at least $5 million. Party A shall be responsible for proper storage and handling after delivery.

7.1 Limitation of Liability: Party B's liability for product defects shall not be capped. For other claims (such as delivery delays), liability is limited to the value of the affected order.

7.2 Indemnification: Each party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other
    party from any claims, damages, or expenses arising from its breach of this
    Agreement or negligent acts.

8. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement:
a) For convenience by providing sixty (60) days written notice
b) Immediately if the other party loses required licenses (DEA, state pharmacy license, etc.)
c) Immediately for cause if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days of notice
d) By Party A immediately if Party B supplies counterfeit or unsafe products

Upon termination, Party A may return unopened products within sixty (60) days for refund, excluding controlled substances which must follow DEA procedures.

8.1 Termination Notice Period: 60 days

8.2 Effect of Termination: Upon termination, each party shall:
    a) Return or destroy all Confidential Information
    b) Pay all outstanding amounts due
    c) Cooperate in transitioning services (if applicable)

9. FORCE MAJEURE

If supply is interrupted by force majeure (including manufacturer shortages, natural disasters, or pandemics), Party B shall: (a) promptly notify Party A, (b) allocate available products fairly among customers, (c) assist Party A in finding alternative suppliers. If shortage continues for more than thirty (30) days, Party A may source products elsewhere without penalty.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois and applicable federal pharmaceutical regulations (DEA, FDA).

10.2 Dispute Resolution: Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall first be
     attempted to be resolved through good faith negotiations. If negotiations fail,
     disputes shall be resolved through expedited arbitration under healthcare industry rules, given the critical nature of pharmaceutical supplies.

10.3 Venue: Exclusive venue for any legal proceedings shall be in Chicago, Illinois.

11. MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between
     the parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

11.2 Amendments: This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

11.3 Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written
     consent of the other party.

11.4 Notices: All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to
     the addresses set forth above.

11.5 Severability: If any provision is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions
     shall continue in full force and effect.
